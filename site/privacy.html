Privacy policy

Calendar agent is a personal tool used only by its owner, on their own laptop. It has no other users.

What it accesses

  • Gmail (read-only): emails with a specific label, to find deadlines, meetings and events. It never sends, changes or deletes email.
  • Google Calendar: to read the owner's calendars for free time, and to add the events, deadlines and study blocks the owner approves.
  • Google Tasks: to add and tick off the owner's to-dos and deadline tasks.

Where data goes

Everything is processed on the owner's laptop, including by a local AI model. Google data is not sent to any other server or service, not sold, not shared, and not used for advertising or to train AI models. Short notifications about the owner's own schedule are sent to the owner's own Telegram chat.

Storage

A small local database on the laptop remembers which emails were already read and the choices the owner made. The owner can delete it at any time, and can revoke the app's access in their Google Account under Security → Third-party access.
